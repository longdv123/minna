3  Write a, an, the or – . Say why you have chosen that article.

1   Someone is coming to fix ……the…… air conditioning tomorrow. (specific – the air conditioning in the flat)

2   I love learning about ……………… wildlife.

3   My sister is mad about ……………… horses.

4   I bought a new phone, but ……………… screen is broken.

5   It’s my dream to climb ……………… Mount Everest.

6   I want to be ……………… university professor one day.

7   I’d love to get ……………… new surfboard.

8   Are you going to ……………… south coast this summer?

9   ……………… horses we rode yesterday were beautiful.

Answer

1   the (specific – the air conditioning in the flat)

2   – (general)

3   – (general)

4   the (mentioned a second time – the screen of the phone)

5   – (mountain)

6   a (job – not an because of /j/ sound)

7   a (mentioned first time)

8   the (only one)

9   The (specifical horses)
