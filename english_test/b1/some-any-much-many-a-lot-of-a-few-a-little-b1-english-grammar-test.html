1   He gave me any advice about taking public transport in the city.   ……some……

3   I haven’t got many to recycle; just a few bottles and old newspapers.   ……………….

4   There’s many noise pollution if you live near the ring road.   ……………….

5   It’s a few cold because of the power cut, which means there’s no heating.   ……………….

6    There are a lot of graffiti on the old train station building.   ……………….

Answer

1   He gave me any advice about taking public transport in the city. some

2   I decided to spend a little days in London and discovered there are much green spaces in the city. a few/some, a lot of

3   I haven’t got many to recycle; just a few bottles and old newspapers. much

4   There’s many noise pollution if you live near the ring road. a lot of

5   It’s a few cold because of the power cut, which means there’s no heating. a little

6   There are a lot of graffiti on the old train station building. is
