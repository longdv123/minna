1  Bold the shorter action in the past that interrupts the action or event that was in progress. Underline what was in progress.

1   The shoplifter ……was trying…… to steal some perfume when the store detective caught her.

2   Our friends were enjoying themselves at the party when we arrived.

3   I saw a well-dressed elderly man vandalising a building when I was walking to school yesterday.

4   While we were swimming in France on our holidays, the pool manager gave my dad a fine for wearing loose-fitting shorts!

5   Did you see anyone suspicious when you were waiting for the bus yesterday?

Answer

1   The shoplifter was trying to steal some perfume when the store detective caught her.

2   Our friends were enjoying themselves at the party when we arrived.

3   I saw a well-dressed elderly man vandalising a building when I was walking to school yesterday.

4   While we were swimming in France on our holidays, the pool manager gave my dad a fine for wearing loose-fitting shorts!

5   Did you see anyone suspicious when you were waiting for the bus yesterday?

2  Decide if the verbs in brackets should be in the past simple (PS) or the past continuous (PC). Write the correct verbs.

1   Mum was making dinner when I (ask) her if I could go and meet my friends.   …PS… ……asked……

2   They (play) tennis when it started to rain.   …… ……………..

3   The thieves (rob) the bank when the police arrived at the crime scene.   …… ……………..

4   A bald, middle-aged burglar (fall) out of a window while he was burgling a house.   …… ……………..

5   The pickpocket who stole my grandpa’s wallet (wear) a cap and a dark coat.   …… ……………..

6   While I was waiting for the show to begin, I (make) myself a drink.   …… ……………..

Answer

1   ask = PS; asked

2   play = PC; were playing

3   rob = PC; were robbing

4   fall = PS; fell

5   wear = PC; was wearing

6   make = PS; made

3  Underline the verb that is wrong in each sentence and write the correct form.

1   I was walking along the road when I ……was finding…… £10.   ……found……

2   While he was deciding which T-shirt to buy, a shoplifter was appearing next to him and put a T-shirt right into her bag!   ………………..

3   We shook with fear when the thieves came out of the bank. We were so afraid!   ………………..

4   My grandma went to the police station because it was holding an identity parade to find the pickpocket who was stealing her bag.   ………………..

5   While he was watching TV, the police were ringing to say they had caught the vandal who damaged his car.   ………………..

Answer

1   was finding; found

2   was appearing; appeared

3   shook; were shaking

4   was stealing; stole

5   were ringing; rang

6  Write the correct the pronoun in each sentence.

1   Hannah bought ……herself…… a new dress.

2   They made …………………. a picnic for the school trip.

3   Luckily. Julian didn’t hurt …………………. when he fell off his bike.
