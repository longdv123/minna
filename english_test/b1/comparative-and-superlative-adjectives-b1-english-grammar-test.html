2  Are these sentences true (T) or false (F)? Correct the mistakes.

1   Zebras are more unusual than cats.   …T…

2   In sport, the winners receive less money than the losers.   ……

3   The Arctic is not as hot as the Sahara.   ……

4   Cushions are cosier than bricks.   ……

5   A kilo of rocks is heavier than a kilo of bread.   ……

6   A pyramid is as wide at the top as at the bottom.   ……

Answer

1   T

2   F – more money

3   T

4   T

5   F – as heavy as

6   F – wider at the bottom / not as wide at the top / narrower at the top

3  Complete with the correct form of the adjective in brackets.

1   Without a radiator, the attic is ……less comfortable…… than the lounge. (comfortable)

2   This bathroom is the …………………. room in the house. (small)

3   Her bedroom is …………………. than mine. (cosy)

4   If it’s …………………. tomorrow than it was today, we’ll go to the beach. (hot)

5   The house was even …………………. than in the photos. (lovely)

6   The kitchen is not as …………………. as the dining room. (big)

7   It’s perfect. It’s the …………………. palace in the country. (beautiful)

Answer

1   less comfortable

2   smallest

3   cosier

4   hotter

5   lovelier

6   big

7   most beautiful

8   coldest

4  Underline and correct the mistakes.

1   Other people say that is better the city.

      ………the city is better……..

2   I think this flat is more smaller than the other one.

      …………………………………………….

3   I think it will be more funnier with your friends.

      …………………………………………….

4   In the afternoon, the weather started getting worst.

      …………………………………………….

5   Since I moved to a new house, I live more further away from school.

      …………………………………………….

6   I think is more relaxing the beach.

      …………………………………………….

Answer

1   is better the city, the city is better

2   more smaller, smaller

3   more funnier, more fun / funnier

4   worst, worse

5   more further, further

6   is more relaxing the beach, the beach is more relaxing
