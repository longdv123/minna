1  Are these sentences correct (✓) or incorrect (✗)? Correct the mistakes.

1   Last night, Lola has watched a gig on her tablet.

2   Wow! Sheila has just given me a concert ticket.

3   I haven’t bought any music this year.

2   ✓

4   ✗ – Frank came

9   ✗ – I didn’t see
