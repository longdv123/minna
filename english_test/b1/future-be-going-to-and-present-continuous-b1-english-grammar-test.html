1  Match the sentences (1–4) to the correct rules (A–D). Then decide if the sentences use the present continuous (PC) or be going to (G).

1   a definite future plan   …C…

2   something you intend to do in the future   ………

3   something you know is likely to happen   ………

4   a definite future plan   ………

A   I’m going to learn the guitar next term.   ………

B   There is going to be a thunderstorm later.   ………

C   I’m travelling to Italy next week.   …PC…

D   I’m not going away with my grandparents this summer.   ………

Answer

1 C; PC   2 A; G   3 B; G   4 D; PC

2  Choose the correct form of the future to complete the sentences. Underline the words that give a specific time reference to help you decide when to use the present continuous.

1   We’re ………………. Alison from the train station at noon.

      A picking up     B going to pick up

2   The flight is ………………. at half past four this afternoon.

      A going to get in     B getting in

3   I’m ………………. Australia one day. It’s my dream!

      A visiting     B going to visit

4   They’re ………………. for Ireland at the weekend.

      A leaving     B going to leave

5   It’s ………………. later. Look at all those clouds!

      A going to rain     B raining

6   Sam is ………………. an architect when he’s older.

      A being     B going to be

Answer

1   picking up; at noon

2   getting in; at half past four this afternoon

3   going to visit

4   leaving; at the weekend

5   going to rain

6   going to be

5  Complete the text about Matthew’s trip with the verbs in the correct form of the present continuous or be going to. Then answer the questions.

Going away by Matthew Cole

I 1……’m going to…… (go) Granada in Spain in the Easter holidays. I 2………………… (learn) Spanish at a language school, and I’m really excited! I 3………………… (stay) with a family for two weeks, and they’ve got a son who I can practise speaking with. I 4………………… (study) Spanish five days a week, and at the weekends I 5………………… (do) some sightseeing in the city. I 6………………… (not look round) every church and museum, but I 7………………… (visit) the Alhambra Palace. Granada isn’t far from the coast, so the family 8………………… (take) me to the beach the first weekend. And it’s near the mountains too, so we 9………………… (go) skiing the following weekend. It 10………………… (be) amazing!

1   Where’s Matthew going at Easter?

      ……He’s going to Granada in Spain. ……

2   Why is he going there?

      …………………………………………..

3   Where’s he staying?

      …………………………………………..

4   Is he going to do some sightseeing?

      …………………………………………..

5   What’s he doing the first weekend?

      …………………………………………..

6   What’s he doing the second weekend?

      …………………………………………..

Answer

1   ’m / am going to

2   ’m / am going to learn

3   ’m / am staying

4   ’m / am going to study

5   ’m / am going to do

6   ’m / am not going to look round

7   ’m / going to visit

8   are taking

9   ’re / are going

10   ’s / is going to be

Suggested answers

1   He’s going to Granada in Spain.

2   He’s going to learn Spanish at a language school.

3   He’s staying with a Spanish family.

4   Yes, he’s going to visit the Alhambra Palace. But he isn’t going to look round every church and museum.

5   He’s going to the beach.

6   He’s going skiing in the mountains.
