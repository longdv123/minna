1  Decide if the sentences use the past simple (PS) or present perfect (PP). Circle the time phrases in the past simple sentences.

1   I’ve never eaten crocodile meat.   …PP…

2   Dad’s ordered a takeaway for dinner.   ……..

3   I once tried snails on holiday in France.   ……..

4   Last weekend, I ate popcorn at the cinema.   ……..

5   Has he ever tasted oysters?   ……..

6   I had the best ice cream when I went to Italy a few years ago.   ……..

1 PP

2 PP

3 PS, (once)

4 PS, (Last weekend)

5 PP
