1  Write will or be going to for the future tense rules.

1   predicting the future based on what we see or know now    ……be going to……

2   a general prediction about the future   ………………….

3   talking about something that’s already been decided   ………………….

4   deciding to do something while speaking   ………………….

Answer

1 be going to

2 will

3 be going to

4 will

2  Decide if the verbs in bold should use will (W) or be going to (G) in the future. Then write the correct form of the verbs.

1   I see a doctor about the nasty cut on my forehead.

      …G…   ……am going to see……

2   Have a good night’s sleep and you feel better in the morning.

      ……..   ………………………………..

3   He catch a cold if he doesn’t wear a coat in this weather.

      ……..   ………………………………..

4   You take some medicine for that awful cough?

      ……..   ………………………………..

5   They not listen to their mother. They’re such naughty children!

      ……..   ………………………………..

Answer

1   G, I am going to see a doctor about the nasty cut on my forehead.

2   W, Have a good night’s sleep and you will feel better in the morning.

3   W, He will catch a cold if he doesn’t wear a coat in this weather.

4   G, Are you going to take some medicine for that awful cough?

5   W, They won’t listen to their mother. They’re such naughty children!

3  Correct the mistakes in bold in each sentence.

1   What time you will come to my house later?   ……will you……

2   Let’s meet at my house at 7 pm and then we’re going to catch the bus into town.   …………………

3   Yesterday, I hurt my ankle playing football. I am not going be able to play in Saturday’s tournament now.   …………………

4   He bought a beautiful bouquet of flowers because he will go and visit his grandma.   …………………

5   I’ll go and buy some medicine for this terrible earache I’ve got.   …………………

Answer

1   will you

2   we’ll catch

3   won’t

4   is going to visit

5   I’m going to buy

4  Match the sentences (1–6). to the responses (A–F). Then complete the responses with the correct form of will or be going to.

1   I’ve got a sore throat.   …D…

2   It’s sunny today.

3   Do you think we’ll live forever?

4   Emma’s got a high temperature.

5   What time is your hospital appointment?

6   Where’s he going on holiday this year?

B   No, we …………………… . We all have to die one day.

C   I …………………… just check.

E    He …………………… go back to Spain.

F    Yes! We …………………… go to the beach.

Answer

2   F   ’re / are going to

3   B   won’t / will not

4   A   ’s / is going to

5   C   ’ll / will

6   E   ’s / is going to

5  Write sentences with will or be going to.

1   If you drink this hot honey and lemon drink, you / feel / better

      ……You’ll feel better. ……

2   This funfair ride is too fast for me, think / be / sick

      …………………………………………….

      …………………………………………….

4   you / help / me / carry / these bags / please / ?

      …………………………………………….

      …………………………………………….

go     get     find     play     study     tell
