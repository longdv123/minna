1  Decide if the sentences contain a Purpose (P), Contrast (C) or Reason (R) clause.

1   I can’t be a pilot as my eyesight isn’t very good.   …R…

2   I’d love to be doctor so that I can help people.   ………

3   I think your career is very important, while my twin brother thinks that family is more important.   ………

4   Although I love science, I want to work in the arts.   ………

5   I don’t want a desk job because I’ll be bored.   ………

6   Whereas some jobs are quite physical, others require brain power.   ………

7   We go on work experience in order to learn what a job involves.   ………

Answer

1 R   2 P   3 C   4 C   5 R   6 C   7 P
