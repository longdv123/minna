1  Complete the direct questions.

1   She asked me where the keys were.

      ……‘Where…… ……are…… ……the…… ……keys?’……

2   She asked me if I wanted to go swimming on Sunday.

      ‘Do …………… …………… …………… …………… …………… on Sunday?’

3   They wanted to know how old I was.

      ‘How …………… …………… you?’

4   The teacher asked if I had done my homework.

      ‘Have …………… …………… …………… homework?’

5   She asked me how often I went bowling.

      ‘How …………… …………… …………… …………… bowling?’

6   She wondered whether we were taking an earlier train.

      ‘Are …………… …………… …………… …………… train?’

Answer

1   ‘Where are the keys?’

2   ‘Do you want to go swimming on Sunday?’

3   ‘How old are you?’

4   ‘Have you done your homework?’

5   ‘How often do you go bowling?’

6   ‘Are you taking an earlier train?’

3  Order the words to make the reported questions.

1   ‘What school do you go to?’ (went / He / to / I / school / me / what / asked)

      ……He asked me what school I went to. ……

2   ‘Are you learning French?’ (French / whether / He / learning / me / I / asked / was)

      ………………………………………………………

3   ‘Where did you put my shoes?’ (put / wanted / know / She / to / shoes / I / had / where / her)

      ………………………………………………………

4   ‘Have you been to Berlin before?’ (The receptionist / Berlin / if / wondered / to / I / before / been / had)

      ………………………………………………………

5   ‘Are you in any sports clubs?’ (was / asked / I / sports / She / if / clubs / in / me / any)

      ………………………………………………………

6   ‘What’s the name of your school?’ (of / me / was / my / asked / The trainer / name / the / school / what)

      ………………………………………………………

Answer

1   He asked me what school I went to.

2   He asked me whether I was learning French.

3   She wanted to know where I had put her shoes.

4   The receptionist wondered if I had been to Berlin before.

5   She asked me if I was in any sports clubs.

6   The trainer asked me what the name of my school was.

4  Underline the mistakes in the reported questions, and correct them.

1   They wanted to know where was the station.   ……where the station was……

2   She asked that if I have time to talk.

3   My dad asked me why I no had phoned him.

4   They wondered whether if I wanted to go skiing next week.

5   My mum asked me if I have had fun yesterday.

6   He asked me where can he buy a USB charger.

Answer

1   They wanted to know where was the station. where the station was

2   She asked that if I have time to talk. if I had

3   My dad asked me why I no had phoned him. hadn’t

4   They wondered whether if I wanted to go skiing next week. whether/if

5   My mum asked me if I have had fun yesterday. had / had had

6   He asked me where can he buy a USB charger. He could/can
