1  Write the regular comparative form of the adjectives by adding –er, –ier or more.

1   easy = ……easier……

2   small = ……………….

3   important = ……………….

4   nice = ……………….

5   busy = ……………….

6   beautiful = ……………….

7   healthy = ……………….

8   old = ……………….

9   expensive = ……………….

10   funny = ……………….

Answer

1   easy = easier

2   small = smaller

3   important = more important

4   nice = nicer

5   busy = busier

6   beautiful = more beautiful

7   healthy = healthier

8   old = older

9   expensive = more expensive

10   funny = funnier
