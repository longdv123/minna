4  Make sentences using have + something + done. Put have in the correct form.

1   Last week ……I had my eyes tested…… . (I / eyes / test)

2   I like ………………………………… . (have / shoulders / massage)

3   Yesterday ………………………………… at the carnival. (we / faces / paint)

4   Tomorrow ………………………………… . (I / hair / cut)

5   When are you going to …………………………………? (computer / fix)

6   Oh no! I’ve ………………………………… . (have / bag / steal)

Answer

1   I had my eyes tested

2   having my shoulders massaged

3   we had our faces painted

4   I’m having / going to have my hair cut

5   have your computer fixed

6   had my bag stolen.

5  Complete the story using have + something + done. Put have in the correct form.

Yesterday I took my tablet to a shop to 1……have the screen repaired…… (the screen, repair). It’s been a disaster from the start. I bought it online two months ago and I 2…………………………………… (it, deliver) to my neighbour’s house because we were going to be on holiday, but when we got back he said it hadn’t arrived, so I phoned the shop and 3…………………………………… (another tablet, send) to my house. This time it arrived no problem. But a week later, we 4…………………………………… (kitchen, extend) and one of the workmen dropped a hammer on it and smashed the screen. That’s not the end of the story. On my way to the shop yesterday, I went to the Image Centre to 5…………………………………… (some photos, print), and someone stole the tablet from my bag! I couldn’t believe it. It’s the first time I 6…………………………………… ever (something, steal) in my life.

Answer

1   have the screen repaired

2   had it delivered

3   had another tablet sent

4   were having our/the kitchen extended (past continuous for setting the scene)

5   have some photos printed

6   ’ve/have had something stolen
