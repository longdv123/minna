C   It isn’t a good idea to do this.   ………………..

2  Read the exam notice. Complete the sentences with a suitable verb. Then circle True or False.

Friday 6th June

Exam preparation

Monday’s English examination starts at 9 am sharp. Please make sure you are on time. Anyone who is late will not be able to sit the exam.

Please bring only your pens and a bottle of water into the examination room. You do not need anything else. There is to be absolutely no talking during the examination. Students who wish to ask a question may put up their hand and wait for assistance from a teacher.

You have the weekend to finish preparing for the exam. It’s a good idea to take this opportunity to do some final revision. Don’t forget to review your vocabulary lists, verbs and grammar tables. Good luck!

1   You ……mustn’t…… be late for the exam.

      A mustn’t     B don’t have to     T   F

2   You ……………. have to arrive until after 9 am.

      A doesn’t     B don’t     T   F

3   You ……………. bring anything to the exam with you.

      A shouldn’t     B should     T   F

4   You ……………. talk in the examination room.

      A haven’t     B mustn’t     T   F

5   You ……………. to put up your hand if you’ve got a question.

      A had     B have     T F

6   You ……………. use your time at the weekend for some final exam preparation.

      A should     B mustn’t     T   F

7   You ……………. study for the exam all weekend.

      A must     B doesn’t have to     T   F

Answer

1 mustn’t, T   2 don’t, F   3 shouldn’t, F

4 mustn’t, T   5 have, T   6 should, T

7 must, F

4  Rewrite the sentences with the correct form of must, have to or should.

1   You aren’t allowed to leave school at lunchtime

      ……You mustn’t leave school at lunchtime. ……

2   It isn’t a good idea to go to bed late.

      …………………………………………..

3   We must prepare for the history project.

      …………………………………………..

4   It’s a good idea to follow your teacher’s advice.

      …………………………………………..

5   It’s really important that Sandy is on time.

      …………………………………………..

6   It isn’t necessary to get on with everyone.

      …………………………………………..

Answer

1   You mustn’t leave school at lunchtime.

2   You shouldn’t go to bed late.

3   We have to prepare for the history project.

4   You should follow your teacher’s advice.

5   Sandy has to / must be on time.

6   You don’t have to get on with everyone.

5  Write negative sentences using the correct form of must, have to or should.

1   We / have to / look after / cousins / last night

      ……We didn’t have to look after our cousins last night. ……

2   You / must / cheat / exams

      ……………………………………….

3   You / should / be / annoyed / your brother

      ……………………………………….

4   Lena / have to / make up / Mary. It was Mary’s fault!

      ……………………………………….

Answer

1   We didn’t have to look after our cousins last night.

2   You mustn’t cheat in exams.

3   You shouldn’t be annoyed with your brother.

4   Lena didn’t have to make up with Mary. It was Mary’s fault!
