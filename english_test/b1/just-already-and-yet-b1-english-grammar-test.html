3  Complete the conversations and write suitable responses with already or yet.

1   A:   Have you bought a birthday present for Jill yet?

      B:   No, ……I haven’t bought anything for her yet. ……

      C:   Yes, …………………………………… . (perfume)

2   A:   Has Oliver called you …………………?

      B:   Yes, …………………………………… . (phone / good news)

3   A:   We’ve ………………… written our English essays.

English Language Course

      B:   Well done! …………………………………… . (I / not start)

4   A:   I’m amazed at what the basketball team has ………………… achieved so far this year.

      B:   Yes, but …………………………………… . That’ll be a tough game! (not play / Lakeside School)

5   A:   Jules hasn’t watched the film …………………… .

      B:   ……………………………………, ( I / see / it) and I think he’ll be scared! What about you? ……………………………………? (you / watch?)

Answer

1   A: Have you bought a birthday present for Jill yet?

      B: No, I haven’t bought anything for her yet.

      C: Yes, I’ve already bought her some perfume.

2   A: Has Oliver called you yet?

      B: Yes, he’s already phoned with the good news.

3   A: We’ve already written our English essays.

      B: Well done! I haven’t started yet.

4   A: I’m amazed at what the basketball team has already achieved so far this year.

      B: Yes, but they haven’t played Lakeside School yet. That’ll be a tough game!

5   A: Jules hasn’t watched the film yet.

      B: I’ve already seen it, and I think he’ll be scared. What about you? Have you watched it yet?
