1  Complete the sentences using which, where, who or whose.

1   Is this the place ……which…… serves that great ice-cream?

2   Freida, ………………… I met last week, is having a barbecue on Friday.

3   This recipe, ………………… anybody can try at home, is a classic.

4   The supermarket ………………… we usually go shopping is closed at the moment.

5   I like toasters ………………… have space for four pieces of toast.

6   Is he the boy ………………… mum goes to my swimming class?

When can you use that instead? ……………………………………

Answer

1   which

2   who

3   which

4   where

5   which

6   whose

You can use that in sentences 1 and 5.

2  Put a relative pronoun (if necessary) and match the sentence halves. There is one extra.

1   The cakes ……– / that / which…… I bought were horrible, but … …D…

2   The tables ………………… are by the window …

3   That’s Harry, the boy ………………… dad …

4   Only use the eggs ………………… are on the top shelf.

5   I used the money ………………… you gave me …

6   The bakery ………………… we usually get our bread is closed, so …

A   owns the Italian restaurant.

B   I got some from the supermarket.

C   to treat my friends to a nice meal.

D   these ones are delicious.

E    The other ones need to go in the bin.

F    the rest of you, stay here.

G   have the best views.

When is it possible to use that? ……………………….

Which sentences don’t need a pronoun? …………………………

Answer

1   – / that / which (D)

2   that / which (G)

3   whose (A)

4   that / which (E)

5   – / that / which (C)

6   where (B)

F is not needed.

That can be used in sentences 1, 2, 4 and 5.

Sentences 1 and 5 don’t need a pronoun.

5  Rewrite the information from Exercise 4 in a single sentence. Use non-defining relative clauses.

1   ……Luis Prieto, who was born in Salamanca, opened his first restaurant when he was 19. ……

2    ……………………………………………………

3    ……………………………………………………

4    ……………………………………………………

5    ……………………………………………………

6    ……………………………………………………

Answer

Some sentences could be rewritten in two ways:

1   Luis Prieto, who was born in Salamanca, opened his first restaurant when he was 19. / Luis Prieto, who opened his first restaurant when he was 19, was born in Salamanca.

2   Cadbury’s, who sold their first chocolate bar in 1847, generates more than $3 billion a year. / Cadbury’s, who generate more than $3 billion a year, sold their first chocolate bar in 1847.

3   Borough Market, where you can buy many types of food, is located in central London.

4   Cinnamon rolls, which are a Scandinavian classic, were first baked in the 1920s. / Cinnamon rolls, which were first baked in the 1920s, are a Scandinavian classic.

5   The Melrose Sushi Cantina, whose customers included Hollywood stars, closed down last week.

6   The world’s largest ever pizza, which was made in Rome, measured 1,261.65 square metres. / The world’s largest ever pizza, which measured 1,261.65 square metres, was made in Rome.
