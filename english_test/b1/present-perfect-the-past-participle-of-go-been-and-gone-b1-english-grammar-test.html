1   I ……have…… never ……had…… a bank account. (have)

5   We ……………… away all our old CDs to the second-hand shop. (give)

7   My brother is coming back from his camping trip because he ……………… of money. (run out)

6  Write present perfect sentences in the positive, negative or question form.

1   Violet / go / skiing

      ✓ ……Violet has been skiing. ……

      ?  (ever) ……Has Violet ever been skiing?……

      ✗ (never) ……Violet has never been skiing. ……

2   We / do / homework

      ✗ …………………………………….

      ✓ …………………………………….

      ?  (you) …………………………………….

3   I / bring / some change / buy / drink

      ✓ …………………………………….

      ?  (you, any) …………………………………….

      ✗ (any) …………………………………….

4   he / ever / win / medal / for swimming

      ?  …………………………………….

      ✓ …………………………………….

      ✗ (never) …………………………………….

Answer

1   Violet has been skiing.

      Has Violet ever been skiing?

      Violet has never been skiing.

2   We haven’t done our homework.

      We’ve done our homework.

      Have you done your homework?

3   I’ve brought some change to buy a drink.

      Have you brought any change to buy a drink?

      I haven’t brought any change to buy a drink.

4   Has he ever won a medal for swimming?

      He’s won a medal for swimming.

      He’s never won a medal for swimming.
