1   The painting ……was stolen…… (steal) from the gallery last night.

2   Yesterday my friend ……………… (tag) me in a terrible photo.

3   The app ……………… (create) in 2017 using high powered software.

4   I sometimes ……………… (block) people if I don’t agree with them.

5   Millions of photos ……………… (post) online every day.

6   The picture ……………… (take down) after many people complained.

7   When the race finished, they ……………… (give) all the money to charity.

1   was stolen

2   tagged

3   was created

4   block

5   are posted

6   was taken down

7   gave
