2  Underline the correct relative pronouns. Then tick (✓) the sentences which can use more than one relative pronoun and write the alternative.

1   Let’s go and see the film that / who won an Oscar for best picture.   …✓… ……which……

2   See that strange-looking house there? It’s which / where that scary horror film was set.   ……   …………….

3   Andy’s the boy who / which was an extra in the TV crime drama on Channel 8.   ……   …………….

4   Joey is the new animation about a boxing kangaroo where / that becomes a superhero.   ……   …………….

5   A trailer is a video clip which / who advertises a new film or TV show.   ……   …………….

6   Meryl Streep is the famous actress which / that stars in the Mamma Mia! films.   ……   …………….

Answer

1   that, which

2   where

3   who, that

4   that, who

5   which, that

6   that, who

3  Underline and correct the mistake in each sentence.

1   Do you remember Cleo, the girl which moved to another town last year?   …who…

2   After a busy day at school, I need a quiet place when I can relax and watch TV or listen to music.   ……..

3   If you come and visit London, I’ll take you to all the places where are really famous.   ……..

4   At the drama school open day, I met a lot of actors which helped me decide that acting is definitely what I want to do.   ……..

5   The park is a good place for a picnic on a hot day because there are plenty of trees where we can sit under.   ……..

Answer

1   which, who

2   when, where

3   where, that / which

4   which, who

5   where, that / which

5  Connect the sentences with a suitable relative pronoun.

1   Martin Scorsese is a famous American director. He’s made more than 24 films.

      ……Martin Scorsese is a famous American director……

      ……who has made more than 24 films. ……

2   A soundtrack is music. It features in a film.

      ……………………………………………………..

3   Dubrovnik is a city in Croatia. They filmed a lot of Games of Thrones episodes there.

      ……………………………………………………..

4   I’m not really into period dramas. They are about life a long time ago.

      ……………………………………………………..

5   He’s the actor from that new action thriller. He’s going to be on the chat show this evening.

      ……………………………………………………..

6   Did you hear about the cave rescue? It was on the news last night.

      ……………………………………………………..

Answer

1   Martin Scorsese is a famous American director who / that has made more than 24 films.

2   A soundtrack is music which / that features in a film.

3   Dubrovnik is a city in Croatia where they filmed a lot of Games of Thrones.

4   I’m not really into period dramas that / which are about life a long time ago.

5   He’s the actor from that new action thriller who / that is going to be on the chat show this evening.

6   Did you hear about the cave rescue which / that was on the news last night?
