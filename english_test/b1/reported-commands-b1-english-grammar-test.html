1  Decide if the sentences are commands (C), requests (R) or advice (A). Underline the words that help you recognise the sentence type.

1   You should close the window.   …A…

2   Do your work.   ……

3   Please brush your teeth.   ……

4   Don’t bite your fingernails.   ……

5   I don’t think you should buy those shoes.   ……

6   Could you turn off the TV, please?   ……

7   You mustn’t be late.   ……

Answer

1   A, You should

2   C, Do

3   R, Please

4   C, Don’t

5   A, I don’t think you should

6   R, Could you … please?

7   C, You mustn’t

Answer

1   ‘Write a poem, please.’ (request)

      ‘Write a poem.’ (command))

2   You should read Leonardo DiCaprio’s biography.’ (advice)

3   ‘Don’t copy my painting. Create one in your own original style.’ (command)

      ‘Please don’t copy my painting. Create one in your own original style.’ (request)

      ‘I don’t think you should copy my painting. It would be better to create one in your own original style.’ (advice)

4   ‘Please read this historical novel.’ (request)

      ‘(You must) read this historical novel.’ (command)

      ‘I really think you should read this historical novel.’ (advice)

5   ‘You should enrol as a stunt double in the new action thriller.’ (advice)

      ‘Please enrol as a stunt double in the new action thriller.’ (request)

6   ‘You mustn’t put yourself in a stressful situation about the end-of-year exams.’ (command)

      ‘Please don’t put yourself in a stressful situation about the end-of-year exams.’ (request)

      ‘Don’t put yourself in a stressful situation about the end-of-year exams.’ (command)

7    ‘You shouldn’t see the film.’ (advice)
