2  Write suitable modals of probability using the words in the box so that the second sentences match the first ones.

can’t     could     might     must

1   It’s definitely not an insect. It ……can’t…… be an insect.

3   It’s definitely dangerous. It ……………. be dangerous.

4   Maybe it’s harmless. It ……………. be harmless.

Answer
