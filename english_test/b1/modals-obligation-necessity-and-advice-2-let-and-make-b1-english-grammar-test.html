1  Choose let or make. Put the verb in the correct form.

1   My parents won’t ……let…… me travel abroad alone until I’m 18.

2   On the plane yesterday, they ………………. us check in our small suitcases, although we didn’t want to.

3   They always ………………. passengers wear their seatbelts until the plane reaches the terminal for safety reasons.

4   Thank you for ………………. me sit next to my friend.

5   They won’t ………………. you take your skateboard on the plane. It’s too big.

6   My dad ………………. me unpack the moment we got to the hotel – I just wanted to get to the beach!

Answer

1   let

2   made

3   make

4   letting

5   let

6   made

2  Underline the correct modal.

1   You have to / shouldn’t have a letter from your parents saying they allow you to travel.

2   You have to / should take a small bag for things like a book and a phone charger.

3   You needn’t / need to pay attention to the screens at the airport so you know which gate to go to.

4   You needn’t / have to check in online – you can go to the desk at the terminal.

5   You should / shouldn’t stay up late the night before you travel.

6   You have to / don’t have to worry, as the cabin crew will look after you.

Answer

1   have to

2   should

3   need to

4   needn’t

5   shouldn’t

6   don’t have to
