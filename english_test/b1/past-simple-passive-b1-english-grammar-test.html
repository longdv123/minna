1  Decide if the sentences are active (A) or passive (P). Underline the past simple passive structure in the sentences where it is used.

1   The picture was used to create an illusion.   …P…

2   We created optical illusions in our paintings.

3   Computers weren’t invented by Bill Gates.

4   This wall art was done by Banksy.

5   Who designed her wedding dress?

6   When was the internet first created?

Answer

1   P, was used

2   A

3   P, weren’t invented by

4   P, was done by

5   A

6   P, was, created

      A was, using     B was, used

      A was called     B was call

      A was born     B were born

3   weren’t introduced

5  Rewrite the active sentences in the passive form.

1   An Armenian probably made the first leather shoe.

      ……The first leather shoe was probably made by an Armenian. ……

      ………………………………………………….

3   People downloaded the song Shape of You by Ed Sheeran the most in 2017.

      ………………………………………………….

      ………………………………………………….

5   France gave the Statue of Liberty to the USA as a present in 1886.

      ………………………………………………….

Answer

1   The first leather shoe was probably made by an Armenian.
