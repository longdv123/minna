5  Decide if the sentences are active (A) or passive (P). Then rewrite the sentences in the active or passive form.

1   This podcast is recommended in my magazine.   …P…

      ……My magazine recommends this podcast. ……    …A…

2   WhatsApp is used by millions of people.

      ……………………………………………..   ……

3   You need a password to access the school website.

      ……………………………………………..   ……

4   People buy hundreds of products on Amazon every second!

      ……………………………………………..   ……

5   All the latest video games are sold in Gamesworld.

      ……………………………………………..   ……

6   My parents don’t allow me to use the internet for more than an hour a day.

      ……………………………………………..   ……

Answer

1   This podcast is recommended in my magazine. P

      My magazine recommends this podcast. A

2   WhatsApp is used by millions of people. P

      Millions of people use WhatsApp. A

3   You need a password to access the school website. A

      A password is needed to access the school website. P

4   People buy hundreds of products on Amazon every second! A

      Hundreds of products are sold on Amazon every second! P

5   All the latest video games are sold in Gameworld. P

      GameWorld sells all the latest video games. A

6   My parents don’t allow me to use the internet for more than an hour a day. A

      I’m not allowed to use the internet for more than an hour a day. P
