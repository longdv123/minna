1  Look at the determiners and mark the sentences correct (✓) or incorrect (✗). Correct the mistakes.

1   I need to buy some new trousers. ✓

2   Most shops have a lot of promotions in January.

3   How many information is on their website?

4   There are no discounts on electronic items.

5   Do you know any good shoe shops?

6   Much people throw away their receipts.

Answer

1   ✓

2   ✓

3   ✗ – How much

4   ✓

5   ✓

6   ✗ – Many people

4  Underline the wrong word in each sentence. Write the correct word.

1   Can you help? I want an advice about buying a computer.   ……some……

2   We spent plenty of times at the shops today.   ………………..

3   This department store offers much discounts in the summer.   ………………..

6  Choose a noun. Then say: Are these a good idea (G) or a bad idea (B), or it depends (D)? Say why.

beach     city     film     hotel     party

restaurant     shopping centre

1   A ……beach…… with some clouds. ……D The clouds can protect you from the sun, but you could feel cold. ……

2   A ………………. where you can eat as much as you want. ……………….……………….……………….

3   A ………………. with no internet. ……………….……………….……………….

4   A ………………. with a lot of shops. ……………….……………….……………….

5   A ………………. with plenty of guests. ……………….……………….……………….

6   A ………………. without any cars. ……………….……………….……………….

7   A ………………. with several sequels. ……………….……………….……………….

Answer

All answers are valid if justified.

Possible answers:

1   beach D – The clouds can protect you, but you could feel cold.

2   restaurant B – People eat too much.

3   hotel G – You can relax more with no distractions.

4   shopping centre / city D – More options, but need lots of time.

5   party / hotel D – More fun (party), but could get out of control.

6   city / beach G – no pollution

7   film D – Some sequels are good, other not.
